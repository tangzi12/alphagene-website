What the predictor does

A fast fold hypothesis—not a finished protein product.

01

Single-sequence prediction

ESMFold estimates a protein's 3D structure directly from its amino acid sequence.

02

Interactive inspection

Rotate, zoom, and compare cartoon, stick, and surface representations in the browser.

03

Not protein design

This predictor folds a sequence you provide. It does not generate a new sequence for a function.

04

Research-use limitation

Predictions do not establish function, binding, safety, efficacy, or an experimentally validated structure.
