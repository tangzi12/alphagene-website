PUBLIC REFERENCE STRUCTURES FOR ALPHAGENE ORGAN BINDING TEACHING VIEWS
Retrieved: 2026-10-18

These are public target/reference-scaffold coordinates, not proprietary AlphaGene
designs, candidate sequences, docking poses, measured interactions or binding-site
disclosures. An animation moving the target and scaffold together is illustrative,
not a molecular-dynamics trajectory or a binding prediction.

Files retain real protein heavy-atom coordinates (N, CA, C, O and available side
chains). Missing atoms/residues are not reconstructed. Hydrogens, DNA, ligands,
waters, ions and other chains are removed. Highest-occupancy alternate conformers
are selected. Source residue IDs are retained; atom serials are renumbered.
Experimental constructs may differ from the reviewed sequence. See sources.
