AlphaGene Labs Classic Binding Atlas — asset attribution
=========================================================

The coordinate files in this folder are public experimental structures downloaded
from the RCSB Protein Data Bank on 2026-08-27. AlphaGene Labs did not generate or
experimentally determine these coordinates.

Each RCSB record links to the relevant primary citation, authors, experimental
details, and data-usage policies. Structure titles, chain mappings, methods, and
resolutions shown by the webpage are derived from the deposited records.

Interpretation boundary
-----------------------
Static experimental coordinates do not establish affinity, selectivity, efficacy,
safety, or real-time binding dynamics. Antibody entries display Fab fragments where
indicated. Receptor entries may contain experimental domains or ectodomains rather
than complete membrane proteins. PDB 8ZNL is an experimental X-ray entry, but its
RCSB primary citation was still listed as "To be published" on the access date.
